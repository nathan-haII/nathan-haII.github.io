Document Scanner

Ontario License Plate Scanner

A classical computer-vision pipeline that finds and reads Ontario license plates in photos, with no deep learning. It localizes the plate with edge detection and connected components, segments characters with adaptive thresholding, and classifies each 20×20 crop with a Support Vector Classifier. A post-processing step uses the 4-letter, 3-digit plate format to fix confusable characters like 0/O and 5/S.
